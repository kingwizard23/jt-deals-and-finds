Install on your phone

  1. iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: open it in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Install app.
  3. Open Snap to Shelf from its new icon, then sign in and add your key here. The installed app keeps its own storage, separate from the browser.
